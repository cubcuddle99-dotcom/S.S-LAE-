Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what buyers ask most.

Why 280 days?

The gestation period of a cow is about 280 days. We sell after this period is complete, so the animal is ready at the right time.

How is the price decided?

The price depends on the animal's breed, age, health and how far along she is. Contact us for the current price of each animal.

How does delivery work?

We agree a delivery date and place with you after you confirm your order. The expected delivery month is shown on each animal above.

How can I buy?

Call or message us on WhatsApp, tell us which animal you want, and we will confirm availability with you.
